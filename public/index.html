Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account or log in?

No. Public content on all four platforms downloads without any login. Only Instagram private accounts and stories need your Instagram sessionid.

How do I get my Instagram sessionid?

Open Instagram in your browser → press F12 → Application tab → Cookies → instagram.com → find sessionid and copy its value into the Login dialog.

Is my session safe?

Yes. Your session cookie lives only in the server's temporary memory (RAM) and is cleared on restart. It is never written to disk or shared.

What quality and formats do I get?

Videos download as MP4 (YouTube high-res may be MKV/WebM after merge), images in their original format, and audio as MP3/M4A. We always fetch the highest quality available.

Why does a multi-photo post sometimes show fewer images?

Some platforms (notably Facebook) only expose the cover image to anonymous requests; the rest sit behind a login. Instagram carousels return every item.

Can I download from private or deleted content?

Only Instagram private accounts you follow (with your session ID). Deleted, expired, or fully private content on any platform can't be retrieved.
